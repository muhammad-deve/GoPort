# Getting help

GoPort is maintained by a small team, so replies are best effort and there's no guaranteed response time.

| You have | Where to go |
|---|---|
| A bug | [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/muhammad-deve/GoPort/issues) |
| A feature request or an idea | [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/muhammad-deve/GoPort/issues). Check [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) first. |
| A security vulnerability | **Don't open an issue.** Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). |
| A problem with your goport.uz account, plan or billing | Email **support@contact.goport.uz** |
| A question about self-hosting | [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/muhammad-deve/GoPort/issues) |

## Reporting a bug

Search the [existing issues](https://github.com/muhammad-deve/GoPort/issues?q=is%3Aissue) first. A new report should include:

- the command you ran
- your OS and how you installed GoPort (Homebrew, Scoop, Chocolatey, the Windows installer or a direct download)
- the output of `goport --version`
- what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and the full error

If the problem is in the request inspector, say which browser you used. Don't paste account tokens, passwords, or captured requests that contain private data.

## Account and billing

- **Tokens:** manage them on the [Tokens](https://goport.uz/dashboard#tokens) page of the dashboard.
- **Plan:** change or cancel it under [Billing](https://goport.uz/dashboard#billing).
- **Anything else:** email support@contact.goport.uz from the address on your account. Never send your password or a token.
